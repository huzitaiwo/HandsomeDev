( 04 / How I work )

A six-step rhythm that keeps the work moving.

The process is not a ceremony. It is a way to make the right thing visible at the right moment.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the people, constraints, and opportunity before making the first screen.

    Context, questions, references
  2. 02

    Define

    Turn ambiguity into a shared direction, a useful structure, and a crisp next step.

    Flows, scope, priorities
  3. 03

    Design

    Give the experience a visual language that is clear, expressive, and ready to scale.

    Wireframes, UI, prototypes
  4. 04

    Build

    Translate intent into resilient frontend or storefront components that behave well in the real world.

    Components, responsive states
  5. 05

    Refine

    Test the details: hierarchy, accessibility, motion, performance, and the moments that make it feel finished.

    QA, polish, edge cases
  6. 06

    Launch

    Ship with clarity, handoff what matters, and leave a foundation that can keep improving.

    Handoff, documentation, next moves
